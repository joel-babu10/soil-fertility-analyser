Garden Spot Guide
Tips for each growing location at home
🌱 Best Spot for Your Monitored Crop
☀️ Rooftop Pots & Grow Bags
Best for ▼
Full-sun vegetables: tomatoes, chillies, brinjal, spinach, coriander, fenugreek, mint. At least 6 hrs of direct sunlight.
Container tips ▼
Use 12–20 litre pots or grow bags. Ensure drainage holes at the bottom. Coconut coir mixed with compost works well as potting mix.
Watering ▼
Rooftop pots dry out faster. Water daily in summer, every 2 days in cooler months. Check soil 2 cm deep before watering.
Sunlight & heat management ▼
Use light-coloured pots to reduce heat absorption. Add a thin layer of mulch (dry leaves or coconut husk) on top to retain moisture. During peak summer, consider shade nets (30–50% shade cloth) from 12–4 PM.
Soil mix recipe ▼
50% red soil + 30% vermicompost + 20% cocopeat. Add a handful of neem cake per pot to deter pests. Refresh top 2 inches every 3 months.
🌿 Balcony Railing Boxes
Best for ▼
Herbs and compact greens: basil, thyme, parsley, lettuce, spring onion, radish. Great for shallow-rooted plants.
Container tips ▼
Use long railing planters (60–80 cm). Line with newspaper before soil to retain moisture. Shallow roots need only 15–20 cm depth.
Watering ▼
Water every 1–2 days. Watch for wind drying. Self-watering boxes with reservoirs are ideal for busy schedules.
Wind protection ▼
High balconies face strong wind stress. Group pots together to create a windbreak effect. Avoid tall, weak-stemmed plants. Use bamboo stakes for support.
Companion planting ideas ▼
Plant basil alongside tomatoes — it repels aphids. Grow marigolds at the edges to deter whiteflies. Mint works well in hanging pots but keep separate as it spreads aggressively.
🪟 Window Sill Beds
Best for ▼
Microgreens, sprouts, small herbs (mint, curry leaf). Low-light tolerant plants. East-facing windows are best.
Container tips ▼
Small trays or repurposed containers work great. Use well-draining potting mix. Rotate pots weekly for even light exposure.
Watering ▼
Mist lightly or bottom-water trays. Avoid waterlogging in small containers — once every 2–3 days is usually enough.
Light supplementation ▼
If natural light is less than 4 hours, use a basic grow light (LED, 2700K warm white) for 6 hours. Even a desk lamp placed 20 cm above seedlings helps germination significantly.
Best crops to start here ▼
Start seeds of coriander, fenugreek, and radish in window trays — they germinate in 3–5 days with minimal care. Once sprouted (10 cm), transfer to balcony or rooftop pots for full growth.
🌍 Ground / Yard Patch
Best for ▼
Root vegetables and larger plants: beans, gourds, cucumber, amaranth, drumstick, papaya. Plants that need deeper soil.
Soil prep ▼
Mix in compost (kitchen waste compost is perfect). Loosen soil 30 cm deep. Add cow dung manure or vermicompost for best results.
Watering ▼
Water every 2–3 days at the base of plants. Mulch with dry leaves to retain moisture and reduce frequency in summer.
Raised bed setup ▼
For small yards, build raised beds (30–45 cm high) with bricks or wooden planks. Fill with a mix of topsoil, compost, and sand. Raised beds warm faster, drain better, and are easier to manage.
Pest management ▼
Spray diluted neem oil (5 ml per litre) every 10 days as a preventive. Place yellow sticky traps for whiteflies. Keep the area weed-free and clear fallen leaves to reduce fungal risks during monsoon.
