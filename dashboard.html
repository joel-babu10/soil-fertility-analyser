Garden Spot Guide
Tips for each growing location at home
☀️ Rooftop Pots & Grow Bags

Best for

Full-sun vegetables: tomatoes, chillies, brinjal, spinach, coriander, fenugreek, mint. At least 6 hrs of direct sunlight.

Container tips

Use 12–20 litre pots or grow bags. Ensure drainage holes at the bottom. Coconut coir mixed with compost works well as potting mix.

Watering

Rooftop pots dry out faster. Water daily in summer, every 2 days in cooler months. Check soil 2 cm deep before watering.

🌿 Balcony Railing Boxes

Best for

Herbs and compact greens: basil, thyme, parsley, lettuce, spring onion, radish. Great for shallow-rooted plants.

Container tips

Use long railing planters (60–80 cm). Line with newspaper before soil to retain moisture. Shallow roots need only 15–20 cm depth.

Watering

Water every 1–2 days. Watch for wind drying. Self-watering boxes with reservoirs are ideal for busy schedules.

🪟 Window Sill Beds

Best for

Microgreens, sprouts, small herbs (mint, curry leaf). Low-light tolerant plants. East-facing windows are best.

Container tips

Small trays or repurposed containers work great. Use well-draining potting mix. Rotate pots weekly for even light exposure.

Watering

Mist lightly or bottom-water trays. Avoid waterlogging in small containers — once every 2–3 days is usually enough.

🌍 Ground / Yard Patch

Best for

Root vegetables and larger plants: beans, gourds, cucumber, amaranth, drumstick, papaya. Plants that need deeper soil.

Soil prep

Mix in compost (kitchen waste compost is perfect). Loosen soil 30 cm deep. Add cow dung manure or vermicompost for best results.

Watering

Water every 2–3 days at the base of plants. Mulch with dry leaves to retain moisture and reduce frequency in summer.
